Privacy policy

Updated 13 November 2026. This policy covers the Samification iOS and iPadOS app operated by Yong Hock Liew.

Try demo

The in-app demo uses fictional records without a staff account or patient game code. Demo progress is saved on your device. Use fictional records in this training release.

What the connected app collects

Staff provide a name and email address when requesting access or signing in. The service assigns account identifiers and records staff access requests, ward membership, approvals and security events.

Staff enter or import admission aliases, medication names, strengths, doses, schedules and instructions. Entering a game code creates a temporary patient identity and links it to the assigned admission. The service records virtual tray submissions, hint and reference use, timestamps, calculated results and clinician review notes.

How information is used and shared

Samification uses this information to sign users in, control access, deliver challenges, calculate scores and preserve evidence for the assigned care team. Approved clinicians can access records within their assigned wards. Paired patients can access the challenge assigned to their admission. Supabase provides authentication and database hosting and may process technical information for service operation and security. Samification does not use connected app data for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Retention and your choices

Signing out or ending a patient session does not erase records on the server. The connected service retains account, training and audit records for operation and review. It currently does not have an automatic deletion period. To ask about access, correction, deletion or withdrawing consent to future optional use, contact edmund_liew@live.com. Include only enough information for us to identify your request. We may need to verify your identity and check whether any records must be kept before responding.

App support

For help with Samification, email edmund_liew@live.com. Include your device model, app version and the steps that led to the issue. Keep passwords, game codes and patient details out of support emails.

For an expired or missing game code, contact the clinician who assigned the challenge. To explore the app with fictional data, choose Try demo on the welcome screen. Open Help in the app for step-by-step instructions.

For questions about your actual medicines or treatment, contact your care team.
